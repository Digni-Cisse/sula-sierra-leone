Expert Advice

Heavy machinery solutions for Sierra Leone projects.

What is the best excavator size for mining in Sierra Leone?

We recommend our 33-ton to 50-ton Engirise excavators for mining in Sierra Leone. Their high breakout force makes them perfect for the iron ore and gold sectors in Tonkolili and Kono.

Do you have spare parts available in Freetown?

Yes. We maintain a dedicated parts hub in Freetown. We stock genuine filters, undercarriage components, and hydraulic seals to ensure rapid turnaround.

Are your machines "Tropicalized" for West African heat?

Yes, every machine Sula Equipment imports is tropicalized. They are configured with high-ambient cooling packages (50°C+) and heavy-duty dust filtration.

Can you provide on-site maintenance in remote districts?

Absolutely. Our field service teams travel to any district in Sierra Leone for on-site servicing, maintenance, and emergency repairs.

Do you offer Rent-to-Own options?

Yes, Sula Equipment offers flexible rental and lease-to-purchase agreements. All options are tailored to the cash flow of Sierra Leonean construction and mining contractors.
