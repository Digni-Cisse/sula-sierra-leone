Service & Support: Frequently Asked Questions

Can you provide on-site maintenance in remote sites like Kenema or Kono?

Absolutely. Sula Equipment field service teams travel to any district in Sierra Leone for on-site servicing, maintenance, and emergency repairs.

Do you stock genuine spare parts in Sierra Leone?

Yes, Sula Equipment stocks genuine spare parts for our full inventory in Sierra Leone. We keep high-movement parts like hydraulic seals, engine filters, and ground-engaging tools (teeth and blades).

Can I rent a machine for a short-term project?

Absolutely. Sula Equipment offers flexible rental agreements starting from one month for the full inventory – excavators, loaders, backhoes, graders, and more.

Are Sula technicians factory-trained?

Yes, every Sula technician undergoes direct factory training with Engirise Africa (the African subsidiary of Engirise). They are specialists in tropicalized engine systems and high-cycle hydraulics.

Do you support machinery in neighboring West African countries?

Yes, we support cross-border projects and regional logistics for Engirise fleets across the wider West African region.
