Sula Proven

Built for the Hard Rock

Our story began in the iron ore corridors and road construction camps of interior Sierra Leone. We witnessed the cost of downtime - the frustration of waiting months for a simple seal or filter.

Sula Equipment was founded to solve the problems of downtime and stranded operations. Today, over 5 years later, we are trusted across the region for machines that mould mountains and support that builds nations.

Our Mission

To eliminate operational downtime in the interior with factory-direct Engirise power and specialized servicing throughout Sierra Leone.

Our Vision

To be the dominant force in West African machinery - redefining equipment reliability from Freetown port to remote mining belts.
