Technical Durability & Precision.

01

Reinforced Construction

The frame is reinforced with a specialized tensioning system and wide tracks. This reduces wear and extends service life on abrasive Sierra Leonean worksites.

02

High-Strength Steel Build

The structure is fabricated from high-strength, wear-resistant steel. This provides the stability needed for mining and heavy infrastructure.

03

Advanced Hydraulic System

A 34 MPa main system pressure ensures precise control and high efficiency during digging and loading operations.

04

Fuel-Efficient Performance

The 124 kW engine is configured to provide strong performance with lower fuel consumption – get better operational economy and eco-efficiency.

What You Can Do with this machine.

Mining & Quarrying (Tonkolili & Kono)

The 23,000 kg weight and reinforced frame provide stability – necessary for overburden removal and crusher loading in the iron ore and gold sectors.

Infrastructure & Roads (Port Loko & Bo)

A 6,480 mm max digging depth and 9,850 mm digging radius means - efficient drainage excavation and large-scale earthworks for highway projects.

Urban Foundations (Freetown)

The EN220 can navigate the steep gradients and tight site layouts of the Freetown peninsula – thanks to a 3,555 mm minimum swing radius and 30° gradeability.

EN220 Frequently Asked Questions

How does the EN220 handle abrasive mining dust?

The EN220 features a reinforced frame and a tensioning system. This is specifically designed to reduce wear from grit and dust, extending undercarriage life in mining corridors.

Is the EN220 stable on steep Sierra Leonean gradients?

Yes. With a 30° gradeability rating and a 4,437 mm track grounding length, the EN220 offers excellent balance on uneven or sloping terrain.

Does Sula stock genuine EN220 parts in Freetown?

Yes. We maintain a local inventory of genuine filters, seals, and high-wear components for the EN220 at our Freetown Hub.
